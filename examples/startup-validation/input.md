## Notes

The startup idea is a lightweight tool for consultants who need to turn messy client discovery calls into project briefs, risks, assumptions, and proposal tasks.

The strongest wedge may be independent consultants who already use AI heavily but still spend hours cleaning up notes after calls. The promise is not generic transcription; it is converting raw notes into reusable project context and proposal material.

Assumptions:

- Consultants feel enough pain after discovery calls to try a new workflow.
- They trust a local-first tool more than a hosted service for client-sensitive notes.
- A reusable Context Pack is more valuable than a polished report in the first version.

Risks:

- Consultants may already have enough value from meeting transcription tools.
- The tool may need CRM and document integrations sooner than expected.
- The output may feel too generic without domain-specific templates.

Validation tasks:

- Interview five consultants about post-call workflows.
- Ask for anonymized discovery notes.
- Generate three sample Context Packs and ask whether they would reuse them.
- Test willingness to pay for local processing and reusable proposal context.
