## Design Context

### Users

GraphCtx is for a solo technical thinker: a founder, researcher, engineer, consultant, or AI power user who uses LLMs for complex research, planning, design, or strategy work. They have long AI conversations or messy notes and want to turn them into structured, reusable context they can inspect, expand, correct, and export.

### Brand Personality

Calm, analytical, structured, local-first, trustworthy, inspectable, minimal, serious but not boring, developer-friendly, and research-workspace oriented. The interface should help the user feel like they are organizing and extending their thinking.

### Aesthetic Direction

The UI should feel like a lightweight research/engineering workspace: somewhere between an analytical research notebook, a graph inspector, and a focused developer tool. The graph should be useful and navigable, not decorative. The interface should balance linear clarity with graph awareness through a stable three-panel workflow: input, graph, inspector/export.

It should not look like a generic colorful mind map, a Miro/FigJam whiteboard, a Notion clone, a chatbot UI, a flashy AI dashboard, or an enterprise product management tool. Avoid glowing AI command-center aesthetics, playful sticky-note clutter, vague magic, and oversized decorative graph visuals.

### Design Principles

- Prefer inspectability over spectacle: show structure, changes, validation, and export paths clearly.
- Keep the graph as a working surface, not a toy canvas.
- Make the main workflow fast and legible: paste, generate, inspect, select, expand, preview, export.
- Use restrained visual hierarchy, dense-but-readable panels, precise controls, and stable dimensions.
- Default to local-first trust: clear state, clear errors, no hidden mutation, and no surprising persistence.
